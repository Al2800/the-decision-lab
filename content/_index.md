This site documents a number of data and AI-related projects that I plan on updating and being more diligent about documenting.

It is currently focused on an **agentic decision laboratory** that uses Fantasy Premier League as a controlled environment for studying how AI agents make decisions under uncertainty — across structured and unstructured data. As much as possible, this is a record of those decisions.

I'm also looking to bring in a longer-running fascination with games and data, and to see how working with structured and unstructured data here can influence other areas of work I do around AI and AI models.

New here? Start with [why FPL is the test environment](/why-an-fpl-decision-lab/), or browse the [blog](/blog/).
