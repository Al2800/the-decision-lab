This site documents one project, built in the open: an **agentic decision laboratory** that uses Fantasy Premier League as a controlled environment for studying how AI agents make decisions under uncertainty.

The aim is reproducibility, not hot takes. Versioned rules, point-in-time data, deterministic scoring, and a public record of what was decided and why.

New here? Start with [why FPL is the test environment](/why-an-fpl-decision-lab/), or browse the [blog](/blog/).
