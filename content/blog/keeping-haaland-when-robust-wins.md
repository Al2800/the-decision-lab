The early T-48h checkpoint on 19 August did two things at once. It still validated the current Haaland-in advisory fifteen on the new packet. And it still left that side behind the no-Haaland robust arm on six-gameweek expected points.

That gap is the interesting bit. Not as a reason to panic-flip the draft on score alone, and not as a reason to pretend the score doesn't matter.

## What the packet actually said

From the published T-48h agent checkpoint (packet `3b6e5ea1…`, observed 19 Aug, sealed locally about seven hours early):

- Haaland-in side still validates: Bruno (C), same 3-4-3, £0.5m ITB.
- Host scores for that side: human_reference 238.61, robust 234.32.
- New no-Haaland robust arm: 255.88 (Rice / Semenyo / Rogers / Anderson; no Haaland).
- Same pattern as 11 August.

Captaincy stayed on packet EP, not Community Shield narrative: Bruno 6.37 > Haaland 5.74. CS updated the Haaland minutes story; it did not automatically move the armband.

## Validate vs win on score

"Validates" here means the Haaland-in fifteen still clears the lab's own checks on the bound packet. It does **not** mean it beats the comparator arms on the objective the robust path is optimising.

The strategy agent is allowed to override that path — it already did on 12 August when it funded Haaland by dropping the Semenyo / premium-DEF stack the deterministic and robust arms preferred. Publishing the override without the score gap would be incomplete. Publishing the score gap as if it settled the thesis would also be incomplete.

## What did not move on T-48h

The named fifteen held from 12 August through the T-48h bind. Boundaries from the checkpoint that stayed put:

- No Tzolis over Wilson on this packet (EP / start_p worse).
- No Bench Boost — Diop / van Ewijk still funding, not a playing fifteen.
- Noise outside the side (Doku calf chatter, Nørgaard %) ignored rather than forced into the XI.

Confidence is medium after Community Shield evidence, with the packet still `live_faithful_degraded`. Approval stays blocked in that state.

## What I want next

The more these packets run, the more useful it looks to post a few team versions side by side — not one locked XV — and break out where the points are being weighted. The ~17 EP gap over the first six weeks is a prompt for that work, not a tip to copy the robust arm blindly.

I'm still the one who would enter any team. This remains advisory.
