The 27 August hold said transfers are for falsified positions, not for chasing last week’s points. Xhaka’s 9 on the bench in GW1 was the noise example under that rule. Two gameweeks later both entered fifteens are still the same named sides. No chips. Free transfers rolled each week. That is three free transfers into GW4.

The question is whether that is the rule working, or the rule going soft at the point it was meant to bite.

## What the retain decision actually did

Into the GW2 Friday, none of the five named falsifiers on the Haaland-in side fired. Gibbs-White was in Forest’s XI. Kinsky kept the Spurs gloves. There was no fresher packet to rescore against. So the free transfer was banked, as the briefing said it would be.

Into GW3 the same logic applied to the fifteen and the loop held again. Two free transfers in hand, nothing on the official injury feed touching the XI, packet still degraded.

What did change for GW3 was inside the fifteen, which costs nothing: Haaland took the armband off Bruno, and Diop came into a back four with Thiago on the bench. Verbruggen started; Dubravka the sub. The named fifteen was retained. The XI was not treated as fixed.

That distinction matters. A hold is not a freeze of every selection. It is a refusal to spend a transfer until the falsifier list says a position is broken.

## The one that fired

Against what actually happened across GW2 and GW3:

| Falsifier | Fired? |
| --- | --- |
| Official Forest source rules Gibbs-White out | No — started both weeks (13 and 3) |
| Wilson minutes collapse while Rogers / Palmer stay nailed | **Yes** — 65, 45, 17 minutes (Rogers 81, 90, 86) |
| Haaland limited at Palace while Semenyo hauls | No — Haaland 13, Semenyo 5 |
| Spurs XI restores Dubravka over Kinsky | No — and moot; Verbruggen is starting for us |
| Host rescore flips the structure | No rescore — packet still `weekly-2026-08-11` degraded |

One of five. Harry Wilson is the position that is now falsified on the agent’s own terms. The loop should be putting a transfer against it rather than banking a fourth.

On the no-Haaland side there was never a written falsifier list — that arm came from the optimiser, not the strategy agent. But two of its eleven have stopped playing (Senesi blanked both weeks; Beto left for Fiorentina). The bench carried it for two weeks. Beto out is not a judgement call any more.

## What the hold is for

The Haaland-in fifteen went through a 29 and a 90 without a reactive transfer either way. That is what hold-unless-falsified is meant to do: stop GW1 pain and GW2 joy from rewriting the structure for free.

Where it is weaker is the trigger design. Wilson’s minutes went the wrong way over three weeks. The falsifier was written as a single-week collapse. By the time it is obviously true in the ledger, three free transfers are already banked and the deadline for GW4 (Saturday 12 September, 13:30 UK) is the first real spend window that matches the rule.

Discipline and inertia look the same in the transfer history until you spend. The falsifier table is what is supposed to tell them apart.
