Two gameweeks since the last note. Both entered 15s went in unchanged for GW2 and again for GW3. No chips. Free transfers rolled each week. The only moves were inside the Haaland-in side: the armband went from Bruno to Haaland for GW3, and the XI went 4-4-2 with Diop in for Thiago. Verbruggen is in goal on both sides now, Dubravka the sub on the Haaland-in one.

Short version: after three gameweeks the Haaland-in side is three points ahead. Haaland-in 184, no-Haaland 181. The T-48h packet had the no-Haaland robust arm 21 points ahead on its six-gameweek score (255.88 against 234.32). Three weeks in, that gap has closed and gone the other way on the last two.

## The retain decision

The 27 Aug hold briefing named the falsifiers that would move the Haaland-in side before the GW2 deadline. None of them fired on Friday. Gibbs-White was in Forest's XI, Kinsky kept the Spurs gloves over Dubravka, and there was no fresher packet to rescore against. So the free transfer was banked, as the briefing said it would be.

Into GW3 the same logic applied to the 15 and the loop held again. Two free transfers in hand, nothing on the official injury feed touching the XI, and the packet still degraded. The agent's line has been that transfers are for falsified positions, not for chasing last week's points. Xhaka's 9 sitting on the bench in GW1 was noise under that rule; it went 6 and 3 in the two weeks since.

What did change for GW3 was inside the 15, which costs nothing: Haaland took the armband off Bruno, and Diop came into a back four with Thiago dropping to the bench. The 15 was retained; the XI wasn't treated as fixed.

That means three free transfers banked into GW4 (deadline Saturday 12 Sep, 13:30 UK). Whether that is discipline or inertia is the question I'll come back to at the end.

## What I take from the models so far

The captain pick has been the one place where the packet numbers were used directly. Bruno over Haaland was a wash in GW1 (both scored 2) and gained 10 in GW2. In GW3 the Haaland-in side went against the packet and captained Haaland; that gained 7. So the packet is +10 when followed and would have been -7 when it was overridden. Too early to read anything into, but it's the first override on record and I want to keep tracking it.

The hold-unless-falsified rule has held the Haaland-in 15 steady through a 29 and a 90 without a reactive transfer either way. That is what it was meant to do. Where it is weaker is that the falsifier list only covers the advisory side and only covers what the agent could see at T-24h. Wilson's minutes went the wrong way over three weeks; the trigger was written as a single-week collapse.

The six-gameweek comparator score put the no-Haaland arm 21 points clear. Three weeks in, it isn't. That is too small a sample to say anything about the scorer, but it is exactly why I entered both.
