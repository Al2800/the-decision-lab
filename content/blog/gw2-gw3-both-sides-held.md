Two gameweeks since the last note and I've let both 15s run as they were. No transfers in either, no chips, free transfers rolling. I did fiddle with the Haaland-in side inside the squad though, I gave Haaland the armband for GW3 instead of Bruno and went 4-4-2 with Diop in and Thiago on the bench. Verbruggen is in goal on both sides now, with Dubravka sitting behind him on the Haaland-in one.

Where that leaves things after three weeks is Haaland-in 184, no-Haaland 181. Bear in mind the T-48h packet had the no-Haaland robust arm 21 points ahead on its six-gameweek score (255.88 against 234.32), so seeing the Haaland-in side edge in front over the last two weeks is interesting, even if I wouldn't read much into it yet.

## The retain decision

Going into the GW2 deadline the 27 Aug hold briefing had set out what would need to happen for it to move the Haaland-in side, and on the Friday none of it did. Gibbs-White was in Forest's XI, Kinsky kept the gloves at Spurs, and there wasn't a fresher packet to rescore against. So I banked the free transfer, which is what the briefing said to do.

Same again going into GW3. Two free transfers in hand, nothing on the official injury feed touching the XI, packet still degraded. The agent has been pretty consistent that transfers are there for positions that have been falsified rather than for chasing whatever scored last week, and I've gone along with it. Xhaka's 9 on the bench in GW1 is the sort of thing that would normally have me reaching for a move, and he's gone 6 and 3 since, so fine.

The bits I did change for GW3 were free. Haaland took the captaincy off Bruno and Diop came into a back four with Thiago dropping out. I'm treating the 15 as the thing the loop owns and the XI and captain as mine to play with week to week, at least for now.

So that's three free transfers banked into GW4 (deadline Saturday 12 Sep, 13:30 UK). I'm not fully sure whether that's discipline or just not getting round to it, more on that further down.

## What I take from the models so far

The captain pick is the one place I've used the packet numbers directly. Bruno over Haaland was a wash in GW1 (both got 2), gained 10 in GW2, and then in GW3 I went against it and captained Haaland, which gained 7. So following the packet is +10 so far and the one time I overrode it, I got 7 out of it. Obviously nothing to read into at three weeks but it is the first override I've made so I want to keep a note of it as we go.

The hold-unless-falsified rule has done what I wanted, which is keep the Haaland-in 15 steady through a 29 and then a 90 without me reacting to either. Where it's weaker is that the falsifier list only exists for the advisory side and only covers what the agent could see at T-24h. Wilson's minutes drifted down over three weeks but the trigger was written as a single-week collapse, so it took a while to formally trip.

And the six-gameweek comparator had the no-Haaland arm 21 points clear. Three weeks in it isn't. Far too small a sample to say anything about the scorer but this is the reason I put both in.
