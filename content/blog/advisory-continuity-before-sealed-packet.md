I published a GW1 advisory fifteen after the 8th, 9th and 10th of August daily strategy runs. The useful fact is not the full table — that is already on the [GW1 note](/gw1-advisory-team/) — it is that the **named fifteen did not change** across those three days.

That kind of stability is easy to over-read. It is continuity under a live bootstrap and daily search loop, not a locked, host-scored decision. There is still no sealed weekly forecast packet bound and rescored against this draft. Confidence stays low on purpose until that is in place.

## What the continuity is claiming

The agent thesis has been consistent: Haaland, Bruno and Gabriel as the spine; cheap defenders that can still return; mid-price midfield; chips held rather than Bench Boost in GW1 on an enabler-heavy bench. Captain / vice stayed Haaland / Bruno on the fixtures named in that note.

I am still reviewing the team myself. Grok has been the primary advisory arm; I want to see what data it is actually using before I treat a run as more than advisory. Community drafts will have influenced the research to some extent — that is worth saying out loud rather than pretending the lab is sealed from the discourse.

## What would break the continuity

The decision changers the primary model has flagged for the next two weeks are the interesting bit:

- Haaland minutes looking limited after Community Shield
- Gabriel rotation news
- Semenyo locking as a weekly starter alongside Haaland
- Spurs goalkeeper situation clarifying (the £4.0 enabler stays until then)
- Brobbey or Calvert-Lewin ownership / minutes forcing a third-forward rebuild that still clears £100.0m legality

If none of those fire, reprinting the XV is noise. If one does, the post should lead with the delta and why confidence moved — not another full fifteen.

## What still has to tighten

The next lab step called out in the GW1 note is binding the deterministic comparators and a sealed weekly packet before deadline. Until then this remains a working advisory fifteen: useful as a baseline for later diffs, not a tipster card.

I will keep publishing as the daily runs move. The point of writing this draft is to separate **stability of a named plan** from **confidence that the plan is scored under a frozen information set**. Those are different claims, and only the second one is what the engine is meant to produce.
