That gap is why both sides went in for GW1. Not as a tipster hedge. As a live run of an open disagreement the sealed packet would not close.

The Haaland-in advisory side that had stuck since 12 August held into the deadline, with one late move: Kinsky in goal, Verbruggen first on the bench. T-48h still had Verbruggen in the XI. Captain stayed Bruno, Haaland vice. No chip.

The other side I entered was the no-Haaland build (Raya; Gabriel, Guéhi, Senesi, Truffert; Bruno, Semenyo, Rice, Anderson; João Pedro, Beto — Bruno / Semenyo). That is the disagreement, not a second “official” advisory XV.

One gameweek is evidence. It is not a verdict on the six-GW thesis the packet was scoring.
